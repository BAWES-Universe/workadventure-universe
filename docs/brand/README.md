# Universe logo

The white Universe logo, kept here for reference. White on a transparent ground, for dark backgrounds.

- `bawes-universe-white.svg`: the logo, the lettermark and UNIVERSE side by side. The loading screen (`play/index.html`, #981) uses it inline, cut to the logo with `viewBox="24 20 634 104"`.
- `bawes-universe-white.png`: the same logo as a picture, 2728 × 576.
- `bawes-lettermark-white.svg`: the lettermark on its own.

The iPhone and iPad launch pictures are drawn from it by `play/scripts/launch-images/generate.py`.
